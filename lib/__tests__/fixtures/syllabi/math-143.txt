Course Overview:
This course continues the study of calculus with three major units: infinite sequences and series, vector algebra in two and three dimensions, and parametric and polar curves. Students will develop both computational fluency and the ability to communicate mathematical reasoning in writing. All work must show full steps — answers without supporting work receive no credit.

  Midterm 2: 25% (Week 8, covers vectors and dot/cross products)
  Final Exam: 35% (comprehensive, mandatory, Week 11)

Grading Scale: A 90–100, B 80–89.9, C 70–79.9, D 60–69.9, F below 60. No rounding.

Quiz 7 – Polar Tangent Lines
Instructions: Find the slope of the tangent line to the polar curve r = 1 + cosθ at θ = π/2. Show all steps in order: (1) convert to parametric form using x = r cosθ and y = r sinθ with r = 1 + cosθ substituted in; (2) apply the product rule to differentiate y with respect to θ to find dy/dθ; (3) apply the product rule to differentiate x with respect to θ to find dx/dθ; (4) form the ratio dy/dθ ÷ dx/dθ; (5) substitute θ = π/2 into the ratio and state the final numerical slope. Label each step clearly.
Points: 10
Rubric:
  - Sets up parametric form x = r cosθ, y = r sinθ with r = 1+cosθ substituted in: 2 pts
  - Applies product rule to find dy/dθ — structure correct regardless of sign: 2 pts
  - Applies product rule to find dx/dθ — structure correct regardless of sign: 2 pts
  - Forms the ratio dy/dθ ÷ dx/dθ correctly from their expressions: 2 pts
  - Substitutes θ = π/2 and states a final numerical slope: 1 pt
  - Work is clearly organized and steps are labeled: 1 pt
