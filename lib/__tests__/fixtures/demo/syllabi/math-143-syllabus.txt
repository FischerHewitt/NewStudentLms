MATH 143 – Calculus III
Instructor: Prof. Montserrat Dabkowski
Lectures: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday 10:10–11:00am
Textbook: James Stewart, Calculus, 8th Edition

Course Overview:
This course continues the study of calculus with three major units: infinite sequences and series, vector algebra in two and three dimensions, and parametric and polar curves. Students will develop both computational fluency and the ability to communicate mathematical reasoning in writing. All work must show full steps — answers without supporting work receive no credit.

Grading Breakdown:
  Homework: 5% (completion-based, submitted on paper, no late work accepted)
  Quizzes: 10% (weekly, 10 pts each, lowest score dropped, submitted as uploaded file, no calculators)
  Midterm 1: 25% (Week 5, covers sequences and series)
  Midterm 2: 25% (Week 8, covers vectors and dot/cross products)
  Final Exam: 35% (comprehensive, mandatory, Week 11)

Grading Scale: A 90–100, B 80–89.9, C 70–79.9, D 60–69.9, F below 60. No rounding.
