Course Overview:
Biology is the scientific study of life. This course is split into three broad units:
(1) Chemistry, Cells, and Energy — atomic structure, macromolecules, cell structure, photosynthesis, cellular respiration
(2) Genetics and Heredity — DNA structure and replication, mitosis and meiosis, Mendelian genetics, gene expression
(3) Evolution and Ecology — natural selection, evidence of evolution, population ecology, community interactions

Even if biology is unrelated to your major, this course will give you the scientific literacy to evaluate biological issues in everyday life. The lab section is a separate grade component and mandatory — missing more than three labs results in an automatic failing grade.

Weekly Schedule:

Unit 1: Chemistry, Cells, and Energy (Weeks 1–4)

Week 1: Introduction and Scientific Method
  - What is biology? Scientific method and experimental design
  - Connect Homework 1 due Sunday (Unit 1, Module 1)
  - Lab 1: Introduction to the microscope and lab safety

Week 2: Chemistry of Life
  - Atoms, bonds, water properties, pH, organic molecules (carbohydrates, lipids, proteins, nucleic acids)
  - Connect Homework 2 due Sunday (Unit 1, Module 2)
  - Lab 2: Macromolecule identification lab — lab notebook due next week
  - Lab notebook writing assignment: describe your observations and what they indicate about each substance tested

Week 3: Cell Structure and Function
  - Prokaryotic vs. eukaryotic cells, organelles, cell membrane structure, transport
  - Connect Homework 3 due Sunday
  - Quiz 1 Sunday (Unit 1, Modules 1–2, 25 pts, timed, online)
  - Lab 3: Cell structure observation lab

Week 4: Energy and Metabolism
  - ATP, enzymes, photosynthesis, cellular respiration
  - Connect Homework 4 due Sunday
  - Midterm Exam 1 Thursday (in class, multiple choice, scantron, 50 pts)
  - Lab 4: Photosynthesis lab — lab notebook due next week
  - Lab notebook writing assignment: explain what your results suggest about the rate of photosynthesis under different light conditions

Unit 2: Genetics and Heredity (Weeks 5–7)

Week 5: DNA Structure and Replication
  - DNA double helix, base pairing, replication process, mutations
  - Connect Homework 5 due Sunday
  - Quiz 2 Sunday (Unit 1 energy topics, 25 pts)
  - Lab Midterm Exam (covers Labs 1–4, 50 pts)

Week 6: Cell Division
  - Mitosis phases and checkpoints, meiosis and genetic variation, comparison of mitosis vs. meiosis
  - Connect Homework 6 due Sunday
  - Lab 5: Mitosis observation lab — lab notebook due next week
  - Lab notebook writing assignment: describe and sketch the stages of mitosis you observed and explain what is happening genetically at each stage

Week 7: Genetics and Heredity
  - Mendelian genetics, Punnett squares, dominance patterns, sex-linked traits, pedigree analysis
  - Connect Homework 7 due Sunday
  - Quiz 3 Sunday (DNA and cell division, 25 pts)
  - Midterm Exam 2 Thursday (in class, multiple choice, scantron, 100 pts, covers Units 1–2)

Unit 3: Evolution and Ecology (Weeks 8–10)

Week 8: Evolution
  - Natural selection, evidence of evolution, speciation, phylogenetics
  - Connect Homework 8 due Sunday
  - Quiz 4 Sunday (Genetics, 25 pts)
  - Lab 6: Hardy-Weinberg simulation lab — lab notebook due next week

Week 9: Population and Community Ecology
  - Population growth models, limiting factors, species interactions, food webs, ecosystem energy flow
  - Connect Homework 9 due Sunday
  - Lab 7: Population ecology field observation — lab notebook due next week
  - Lab notebook writing assignment: describe the population you observed and apply one population growth concept from lecture to explain its dynamics

Week 10: Human Biology and Course Wrap-Up

  - Connect Homework 10 due Sunday
  - Quiz 5 Sunday (Evolution and ecology, 25 pts — lowest quiz dropped)

Week 11: Final Exam Week
  - Final Exam Thursday (comprehensive, 200 pts, multiple choice + short answer, in class)
