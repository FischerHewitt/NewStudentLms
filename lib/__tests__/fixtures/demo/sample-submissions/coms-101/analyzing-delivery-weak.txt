The speech I watched was a well-known political speech and the speaker did a good job with their delivery. I could tell they had practiced and were comfortable in front of an audience.

One thing I noticed was that the speaker used good eye contact. They looked at the audience a lot instead of just reading from their notes, which helped make the speech feel more engaging and personal. Eye contact is important in public speaking because it builds trust with the audience.

Another thing I noticed was the speaker's vocal variety. Their voice went up and down throughout the speech which kept it interesting. If the speaker had talked in a flat tone the whole time, it would have been boring and hard to pay attention to. Vocal variety is one of the things we learned about in class and this speaker did a good job with it.

The pacing was also good. The speaker did not talk too fast or too slow, which made it easy to follow along. There were some moments when they slowed down to make a point which was effective.

Overall I think this was a strong speech and the delivery was very effective. The speaker communicated their message clearly and used several of the techniques we have studied in class. I think we could all learn from watching speeches like this one when preparing our own. The energy the speaker brought to the performance was something I want to try to bring to my own Round 3 speech.
