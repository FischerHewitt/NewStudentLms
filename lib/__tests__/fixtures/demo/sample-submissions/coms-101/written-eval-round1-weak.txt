I thought the speech was pretty good overall. The speaker seemed prepared and knew what they were talking about. The organization was clear and it was easy to follow along from beginning to end.

The delivery was comfortable and the speaker did not seem too nervous. Eye contact was decent and they did not read directly off their notes the whole time, which was good. The vocal variety could have been better in some places because the speaker used about the same tone throughout most of the speech.

The content was interesting and personal which made it easy to stay engaged. I liked that the speaker shared real experiences rather than just talking about general things. It made the speech feel genuine.

One area to work on would be the pacing at the beginning, which felt a little rushed. Also the conclusion could have been stronger. Overall though it was a solid first speech and I think with some practice the speaker will do really well in the next round. I am looking forward to seeing the improvement from Round 1 to Round 2.
