The scientific method has several steps. First you ask a question, then you form a hypothesis, which is basically your best guess about the answer. Then you do an experiment to test it, collect data, and then look at your results to see if your hypothesis was right. An example would be testing whether fertilizer makes plants grow faster. You would grow some plants with fertilizer and some without and compare them at the end.

Prokaryotic cells are simple cells without a nucleus. Bacteria are prokaryotes. Eukaryotic cells are more complex and have a nucleus where the DNA is stored. Humans are eukaryotes, and so are plants and animals. The main difference is the nucleus and that eukaryotic cells have more organelles inside them.
