In Unit 1 I thought that learning about cells was interesting. I liked learning about the different organelles and what they do. The mitochondria being the powerhouse of the cell is something I had heard before but now I actually understand why. It was cool to see how photosynthesis and cellular respiration are kind of opposite processes and how they both involve moving electrons around. I think the most surprising thing was just how complicated a single cell is and how many things have to work together just for it to stay alive.

For Unit 2, genetics was probably my favorite unit. I liked doing Punnett squares because they were like puzzles and it was satisfying when the ratios came out right. The meiosis material was harder for me but I thought it was interesting how crossing over increases genetic variation. I did not have a very strong personal connection to this material but I understand it better now than I did before.

Overall this class covered a lot of material and I feel like I have a better understanding of biology than I did at the start of the quarter. The labs were helpful for seeing the concepts in action, especially the microscopy labs where we could actually see cells. I would have liked more time to go deeper into some of the topics but I understand that is hard to do in one quarter.
